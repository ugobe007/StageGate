## Trade Show Logistics Breakpoints (Where Robot Companies Struggle)
1. **Shipping & freight** — robots are heavy, fragile, and classified as "machinery" for customs. Wrong HS codes = delays. Batteries = hazmat rules.
2. **Customs clearance** — ATA carnet for temporary import, wrong paperwork = robot stuck at port
3. **Crating & packaging** — most robot companies don't have proper road cases, robot arrives damaged
4. **On-site staging** — who sets up the robot? Most companies send 1-2 engineers who are also doing demos all day
5. **Power requirements** — robots need specific voltage/amperage, show floor power is unreliable, need UPS
6. **Demo environment** — floor surfaces matter (carpet vs. hard floor), lighting affects vision systems, crowd control
7. **Maintenance & repair** — something always breaks. Who has parts? Who can fix it on-site?
8. **Return shipping** — after the show, robot needs to get home. Often neglected in planning.
9. **Off-floor demos** — many customers want a private demo away from the noisy show floor. No venue = no deal.
10. **Warehouse storage** — robot arrives days before the show, needs secure storage
